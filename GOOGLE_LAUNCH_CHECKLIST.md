## Before Search Console / AdSense
- Replace placeholder contact information with real publisher details.
- Culinary-test and improve mass starter recipes before giving each one an indexable URL.
- Add original food photos; update Recipe JSON-LD with image URLs.
- Validate representative recipe pages in Google's Rich Results Test.
- Add property in Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml.
- When AdSense is actually approved, use the real ads.txt publisher ID supplied by Google.
- Configure a Google-certified CMP / Google Privacy & messaging as required for EEA/UK/Switzerland personalized ads.
- Keep ads away from primary search controls and navigation.

No ranking or AdSense approval can be guaranteed.
